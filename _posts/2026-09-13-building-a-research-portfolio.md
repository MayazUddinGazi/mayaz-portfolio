title: "Building a Research Portfolio Around Evidence"
excerpt: "Why I am organizing my experimental, computational, and publication work in one transparent research record."
category: "Research practice"
---

A research portfolio should do more than collect titles. It should show how questions, methods, evidence, and outputs connect. I built this site to keep those connections visible across my work on sustainable cementitious materials and machine learning.

My current research moves between laboratory measurements and computational models. Strength, shrinkage, electrical response, thermal analysis, diffraction, and microscopy each describe a different part of a cementitious system. Machine learning can help combine these signals, but a model is useful only when its assumptions and validation remain defensible.

This portfolio will therefore serve as a living record of publications, conference papers, ongoing projects, and research notes. Future posts will focus on methods I am actively learning or applying, limitations that matter in interpretation, and reproducible ways to communicate engineering data.
